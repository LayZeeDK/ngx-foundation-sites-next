## Answer

Gist: CSS guidance first, plus an optional ResizeObserver directive pair. The spec's first recommendation needs no directive: boxes in XY grid cells equalize with Foundation's own classes (`.flex-container.flex-dir-column` on the cell, `.flex-child-grow` on the box), wrapped block-grid lines equalize separately (Foundation's `equalizeByRow`), and the consumer's own `grid-auto-rows: 1fr` or `subgrid` rules cover one height across rows and aligned card parts; all of it is equal in server HTML, without JavaScript, and in `hydrate never`. For markup CSS cannot reach (the float grid, non-grid lists, content that cannot be restructured) `[nfsEqualizer]` on the container and `[nfsEqualizerWatch]` on each box keep Foundation's three Options (`equalizeOn` through `NfsMediaQuery.is()`, `equalizeOnStack`, `equalizeByRow`), register through `nfsEqualizerToken` with `skipSelf`, observe sizes with one `ResizeObserver`, and in an `afterRenderEffect` `mixedReadWrite` pass write inline `min-height` on every watched element except the tallest of its row; `equalized` reports the row heights; `equalize()` forces a pass. Spec: [specs/equalizer.md](../specs/equalizer.md).

Source abbreviations: FS = `d:/projects/github/foundation/foundation-sites` (6.9.0), NG = `d:/projects/github/angular/angular` (22.2.x), NGC = `d:/projects/github/angular/components` (22.2.x), R: = this effort's `research/` files, BB = `building-blocks.md`, WF = the `web-features` data package (fetched 2026-09-26 from unpkg).

### Decision log

Round 1 (no prerequisites: the Foundation contract and upstream decisions).

1. Q: What are the real Options, events, and methods? A: `Equalizer.defaults` has exactly `equalizeOnStack` (false), `equalizeByRow` (false), `equalizeOn` (`''`); events `preequalized`, `postequalized`, `preequalizedrow`, `postequalizedrow` (plus lifecycle); public methods `getHeights`, `getHeightsByRow`, `applyHeight`, `applyHeightByRow`; group names on `data-equalizer`/`data-equalizer-watch`; no Sass partial, no CSS class. The ticket's option list is right; its `data-equalizer-watch` entry is the watch attribute, not an Option (audit 0001 H6 guard applied). Source: FS `js/foundation.equalizer.js:35-68, 145-236, 246-276, 291-313`; `docs/pages/equalizer.md`; R:`foundation-inventory-forms-media.md` Equalizer.
2. Q: What re-triggers Foundation's pass? A: `resizeme` (window resize debounced 250 ms through `data-resize`), `mutateme` (child-list or `style` mutations in the subtree through `data-mutate`, also fired by Tabs, Toggler, ResponsiveToggle after showing content), `onImagesLoaded` before the first pass, `changed.zf.mediaquery`, and a nested group's bubbled `postequalized` (a container with a nested group listens only to that). Source: FS `foundation.equalizer.js:42-66, 103-112`; R:`foundation-utilities-conventions.md` 2.3 and ImageLoader.
3. Q: Upstream constraints? A: Directive-first (ADR 0001); breakpoint Options keep viewport semantics through `NfsMediaQuery.is()` (ADR 0005, audit 0002 L4); the service is live in the `earlyRead` phase of the first render (ADR 0014); every browser-only read, write, and observer lives in render callbacks (ADR 0008); no library `styles`, Sass subsection shape (ADR 0012, BB 1.13); outputs as Completion outputs, `preequalized/postequalized` -> `equalized` (BB 1.4); one Defaults token per Plugin (BB 1.4); Table B requires the spec to justify the directive next to CSS. Source: the ADRs; BB 1.4, 1.5, 1.7, 1.9, 1.11, Tables A and B Equalizer.
4. Q: Material counterpart? A: None; the Material reference research has no equal-height entry, and Material's card layouts rely on CSS. Source: R:`angular-material-reference.md` (searched, no match).

Round 2 (does the directive earn its place).

5. Q: What does CSS already equalize? A: (a) Foundation's XY grid is `display: flex; flex-flow: row wrap` with default stretch, so cells in one line share a height; a box inside a stretched cell fills it with `.flex-container.flex-dir-column` on the cell and `.flex-child-grow` (`flex: 1 0 auto`) on the box; a `.card` already has `flex-grow: 1` and a flex column under `$global-flexbox`. (b) Wrapping lines stretch separately: that is `equalizeByRow`. (c) `grid-auto-rows: 1fr` in an auto-height grid sizes every row to the tallest (CSS Grid 1, "find the size of an fr" with indefinite free space): non-by-row and `equalizeOnStack`. (d) `subgrid` aligns parts across cards (the nested case). (e) Cells stack below their breakpoint, so `equalizeOn="medium"` with `equalizeOnStack` off is the default flex behaviour. Source: FS `scss/xy-grid/_grid.scss:35-36`, `scss/components/_flex.scss:18-40`, `scss/components/_card.scss:57-60`, `docs/pages/flexbox-utilities.md:81-86, 187-208`, `docs/pages/flex-grid.md:266, 390`; R:`web-platform-features.md` Plugin table Equalizer row and ResizeObserver section.
6. Q: Are those features in the Browser target? A: Yes: flexbox (widely available 2018), grid (2020), `subgrid` (newly 2023-09-15, widely 2026-03-15; Chrome and Edge 117, Firefox 71, Safari 16), `ResizeObserver` (widely 2023-01-28). Container queries are in target but not used for the gate. Source: WF `subgrid`, `grid`, `flexbox`, `resize-observer`, `container-queries`; map Browser target.
7. Q: What residue still needs JavaScript? A: Boxes that are not items (or filling descendants) of one flex line or grid and whose markup cannot be restructured: Foundation 6.9's legacy float grid (`foundation-grid`), inline-block lists, CMS markup, boxes at unrelated depths; and one height across wrapped lines while keeping the flex-based XY grid. Not reasons for a directive: `equalizeByRow` (flex wrap does it), content that changes after load and images (CSS relayouts live), and the breakpoint gate (cells stack). Source: decisions 5 and 6; FS `docs/pages/equalizer.md:100-123`.
8. Q: A directive, or "CSS guidance plus an optional ResizeObserver directive"? A: The latter. The spec leads with CSS, tests the recipes as stories, and keeps a small optional pair for the residue, because a drop-in `data-equalizer` counterpart lets float-grid pages move without a layout rewrite. The lazier alternative (guidance only) is OPEN FOR HUMAN. Source: decision 7; BB Table B "Open risks"; map Destination.

Round 3 (directive shape).

9. Q: Which elements and selectors? A: `[nfsEqualizer]` (`NfsEqualizer`) on the container, `[nfsEqualizerWatch]` (`NfsEqualizerWatch`) on each box; no Structural class exists, so names follow the Plugin (BB 1.3); `exportAs: 'nfsEqualizer'`; entry point `ngx-foundation-sites/equalizer`. Source: BB 1.3, Table A; ADR 0009.
10. Q: How do watches find their group, and what replaces the names? A: Parent-owned registration: `nfsEqualizerToken` (`InjectionToken<NfsEqualizer>`, type-only import) provided with `useExisting`; watches `inject(..., {optional: true, skipSelf: true})`, register at construction, unregister through `DestroyRef`. `skipSelf` makes Foundation's shared element (`data-equalizer-watch="foo" data-equalizer="bar"`) join the outer group while its children join the inner one. Names are dropped; an orphan watch warns once in development. Source: BB 1.9; FS `docs/pages/equalizer.md:42-69`; Angular hierarchical injectors (`skipSelf` starts at the parent element injector); AGENTS.md Content Projection and DI.
11. Q: Do nested equalizers re-provide the token as `undefined`? A: No. The inner equalizer's own provider shadows the outer one, which is all nesting needs; the `CdkAccordionItem` `undefined` pattern (BB 1.9, Table B) serves items that are not containers, and an `undefined` provider on the watch would collide with the inner equalizer's provider on the shared element. Proposed as a building-blocks change. Source: decision 10; BB 1.9.
12. Q: Does registration order matter (sorted collection)? A: No; the algorithm is order-independent (decision 17), so an unordered signal set suffices and no MutationObserver sort is needed. Source: BB 1.9 (sorting applies to ordered children); FS `foundation.equalizer.js:163-168, 189-213` (the only order-dependent code).
13. Q: Inputs, models, outputs, methods? A: `equalizeOn: string` (default `''`), `equalizeOnStack` and `equalizeByRow` (`booleanAttribute`, default `false`), seeded by `nfsEqualizerDefaultsToken`; no model (no two-way state); `equalized: readonly number[]` (row heights top to bottom; `[]` when cleared), emitted only when the result changes, inside `untracked()`; `equalize()` requests a pass; `register`/`unregister` public for the watch. Foundation's measuring methods and per-row events are dropped. Source: FS defaults and events; BB 1.4; the angular-developer skill references `inputs.md`, `outputs.md`, `effects.md`.
14. Q: `height` or `min-height`, and on which elements? A: `min-height` on every watched element except the first tallest of its row, which keeps its natural height. `height` clips content that grows after the pass and hides growth from a `ResizeObserver`; `min-height` lets growth show as a size change, and the untouched tallest lets shrinkage show. Delta: percentage heights inside a box no longer resolve; a flex column replaces them. Proposed ADR. Source: FS `foundation.equalizer.js:229, 264`; the ResizeObserver specification (notifies only on observed box size changes); WCAG 2.2 AA 1.4.4, 1.4.12.
15. Q: What does the observer watch, and where is it created? A: One `ResizeObserver` with `{box: 'border-box'}` on every registered element and on the container (which catches re-wrapping of fixed-width items), created on the first run of the render effect (never on the server), observing nothing while the gate is off, disconnected on destroy. No MutationObserver, no image loader: an image, font, or panel that matters changes an observed size. CDK has no public fit (`ContentObserver` is a MutationObserver; `SharedResizeObserver` is in the private `@angular/cdk/observers/private` entry point). Source: R:`web-platform-features.md` ResizeObserver; R:`angular-cdk-inventory.md` observers; NGC `src/cdk/observers/private/shared-resize-observer.ts:13-29, 97`; BB Part 3 "ImageLoader".
16. Q: Where do the writes happen? A: Never in the observer callback, which only records a zero-size container and bumps a version signal; the pass runs in `afterRenderEffect` `mixedReadWrite` (reset, measure once, apply). Writing in the callback raises the "ResizeObserver loop completed with undelivered notifications" `ErrorEvent`, and `provideBrowserGlobalErrorListeners()` forwards window errors to `ErrorHandler`; a signal an `afterRenderEffect` tracks notifies the scheduler, so the pass runs in the next render. `mixedReadWrite` because write-read-write in one pass avoids painting the reset; one forced layout per pass where Foundation forced one per element. Cost: a post-load change can paint unequal for one frame. This departs from BB Table A's "writes batched in the `write` phase" (proposed change). Source: NG `packages/core/src/render3/reactivity/after_render_effect.ts:103-119`, `packages/core/src/error_handler.ts:221, 249`; the Angular lifecycle guide's phase table; R:`web-platform-features.md` ResizeObserver known gaps; NGC shared resize observer loop handler.
17. Q: How are rows and "stacked" defined? A: Rows by `getBoundingClientRect().top` rounded to whole px, in any order; "stacked" means every row holds one watched element. Foundation compares the first two in DOM order and groups consecutive runs; both break under reorders and sub-pixel tops. Heights by `offsetHeight` after one batched reset (Foundation's measure), assuming Foundation's global `border-box`. Source: FS `foundation.equalizer.js:163-168, 175-213`; FS `docs/pages/equalizer.md:106-110`.
18. Q: Hidden groups (inside a closed tab or Toggler)? A: A container last observed at zero size skips the pass and keeps its heights; showing it changes its size and runs a pass, so Tabs, Toggler, and ResponsiveToggle need not notify anyone. Source: R:`foundation-utilities-conventions.md` 2.3.
19. Q: Does it converge, including nested groups? A: A pass that changes heights causes one more observer report; the next pass writes the same values and emits nothing; then no size changes. The first pass runs before the observer's first report, so client rendering is equal at first paint. Inner writes change an outer watched element's size, running the outer pass. Asserted by browser-level tests; no prototype. Source: the ResizeObserver specification (comparison with the last reported size); decisions 14 to 16.
20. Q: `equalizeOn` semantics and timing? A: Reactive read of `mq.is(equalizeOn())` in the pass; `''` and `all` are `true`; an unknown name warns and answers `false` (the service's rule); the first pass sees the live breakpoint (ADR 0014). Source: [Spec: Breakpoint service (shared utility)](53-spec-breakpoint-service.md) decisions 3 and 17; ADR 0005; ADR 0014; audit 0002 L4.

Round 4 (rendering modes, accessibility, styling, testing).

21. Q: Rendering modes? A: Server: the directives render nothing, no inline style; hydration claims the nodes unchanged and the first pass writes heights (a shift, documented; the CSS answer has none); container and watches share one Hydration boundary, but a watch in a nested `@defer` joins when it renders; `hydrate never` keeps natural heights (the CSS answer works there); no listeners, so no `jsaction` and no replay; prerendering equals SSR; zoneless. Source: ADR 0008; BB 1.11 decisions 3, 6, 7; R:`angular-rendering-modes.md` section 7 rules 1 to 11 and the Equalizer checklist row.
22. Q: ARIA and keyboard? A: None (layout only; DOM order untouched). Source: R:`foundation-inventory-forms-media.md` Equalizer, Keyboard and ARIA behaviour; R:`aria-apg-patterns.md`.
23. Q: WCAG 2.2 AA 1.4.4 Resize Text (200 percent)? A: Requirement, met by construction: the CSS answer never fixes a height; the directive writes only `min-height` and re-equalizes on growth. Playwright proves it at root font size 200 percent and at a 640 px viewport. Foundation's own Equalizer fails it (fixed `height`), which is the reason for decision 14. Source: WCAG 2.2 1.4.4; FS `foundation.equalizer.js:229`.
24. Q: WCAG 2.2 AA 1.4.10 Reflow (320 CSS px)? A: Requirement: the XY grid stacks below `medium`; the grid recipes are single-column at the Zero breakpoint and add columns only inside `breakpoint(medium)` (the spec's subgrid example is written that way for this reason); the directive writes nothing when gated off or stacked and never causes horizontal scrolling. Playwright at 320 px asserts no sideways scroll and no overflow. Source: WCAG 2.2 1.4.10; FS `docs/pages/xy-grid.md`.
25. Q: WCAG 2.2 AA 1.4.12 Text Spacing? A: Requirement, met as 1.4.4; Playwright injects the text-spacing stylesheet. Source: WCAG 2.2 1.4.12.
26. Q: WCAG 2.2 AA 1.3.2 Meaningful Sequence and 2.4.3 Focus Order? A: Requirement: the directive never reorders; the recipes use source-order auto placement and the docs forbid `order`, `grid-auto-flow: dense`, and reordering placement; stories assert the DOM order is unchanged. Foundation's Sass defaults pass as used; no setting or custom rule is needed. The story gate is axe with the WCAG 2.2 AA tag set (`wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag21a`, `wcag21aa`, `wcag22aa`); clipping and reflow are Playwright's, because axe cannot see them. Source: WCAG 2.2 1.3.2, 2.4.3; BB 1.10; user rule restated 2026-09-26.
27. Q: Animation? A: None; instant `min-height`; no reduced-motion rule needed; a consumer transition on the height is unsupported. Source: ADR 0003; BB 1.6.
28. Q: Sass and custom CSS? A: No library CSS and no `nfs-equalizer` mixin; the CSS answer uses Foundation's `foundation-xy-grid-classes`, `foundation-flex-classes`, `foundation-callout`, `foundation-card` (and `foundation-grid` for the residue) plus the consumer's own grid rules built with `breakpoint()`. The directive owns the inline `min-height` of watched elements; consumer minimums go in stylesheets. Source: ADR 0012; BB 1.13; [Sass packaging for the new library](57-sass-packaging.md) answer.
29. Q: Testing seams? A: Stories for the four CSS recipes and seven directive cases (breakpoint-independent `small-*` layouts, because the test runner's viewport is narrow); browser-level tests for pass results, the stacked rule, the gate through a fake `MediaMatcher`, emission, registration, nesting, observer hygiene (no window `error` events), hidden containers, `equalize()`, and the Defaults token; a node-level SSR smoke under the library's unit-test target (the test seam prototype's route) plus a table-tested pure planner; Playwright for breakpoints, by-row regrouping, 1.4.4, 1.4.10, 1.4.12, hydration, and dehydrated and `hydrate never` blocks. A: Group names, `data-resize`/`data-mutate`, the debounced resize, `mutateme`, `onImagesLoaded`, inline `height`, bubbled nested coordination, the first-two stacked check and consecutive row grouping, the pre and row events, the four measuring methods, the height reset on destroy (a directive dies with its element), and the `.foundation-mq` handshake. ## Prototype needed

None. Every design question was settled from sources: the write deferral is forced by the ResizeObserver loop error regardless of timing, convergence follows from the ResizeObserver size comparison, and the remaining behaviours (convergence counts, no window errors, the one-frame lag) are asserted by the browser-level and Playwright tests in the spec rather than being open design choices.

### Proposed glossary terms

**Watched element**:
An element whose height an Equalizer matches to the tallest element of its row; the replacement for Foundation's `data-equalizer-watch` element.
_Avoid_: watch, equalized item, equalizer child

### Proposed ADR

`adr/0021-equalizer-min-height.md`: "Equalizer is CSS layout first; its optional directive writes `min-height` on every watched element except the tallest of its row". It meets the bar: hard to reverse (consumers' CSS will depend on `min-height` versus `height`), surprising without context (the tallest element is skipped on purpose, and Foundation writes `height`), and the result of a real trade-off (Foundation's `height` plus MutationObserver and image loader, `min-height` on every element, writes inside the observer callback).
