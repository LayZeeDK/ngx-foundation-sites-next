## Question

What is the API of the shared anchored-pane utility that Dropdown and Tooltip position and dismiss through, and what does its spec say?

## Answer

Spec: [specs/anchored-pane.md](../specs/anchored-pane.md). Resolved 2026-09-26 by self-grilling (AFK), both sides played against the sources below, under the user's WCAG 2.2 AA rule and the triage rule for human-only items.

Gist: one secondary entry point, `ngx-foundation-sites/anchored-pane`, with three parts and no directive, component, token, Defaults token, provider function, or CSS. The Positioner, `nfsPositioner(options)`, is an injection-context function that ports Foundation's Positionable and Box (12 placements, offsets, pip, the 12-candidate search with least overlap, the body-box Collision bound, RTL through CDK `Directionality`), measures once in the `afterRenderEffect` `earlyRead` phase, writes inline `top`/`left` with `Renderer2` in `write`, re-runs from a `ResizeObserver` on pane, anchor, and bound while open and from scroll (`followScroll`, on by default, re-offsets without re-searching), and returns `{placement, reposition()}`; the formulas are exported as pure functions, which DropdownMenu's `opens-*` check reuses. The Light dismiss registry, `NfsLightDismiss` (`@Service()`, the one piece with shared state), with the helper `nfsLightDismiss(options)`, follows the HTML popover light-dismiss model: outside press decided on `pointerup` after an outside `pointerdown`, Triggers and open descendants inside, ancestry by containment of pane or Trigger, Escape on the topmost entry only, focus moving outside, and one-open-at-a-time groups replacing `closeme.zf.*`; close reasons `'click' | 'keydown' | 'tab' | 'sibling'`. The hover-intent helper, `nfsHoverIntent(options)`, opens and closes after delays on code-added `pointerenter`/`pointerleave` over the Triggers plus the pane, ignores touch pointers per event, floors the close delay at 100 ms, and never auto-hides. CDK Overlay is not used and is no longer ADR 0002's fallback.

### Decision log

Sources: FS = the Foundation 6.9.0 clone; NG = the Angular 22.2.x clone; NGC = the components 22.2.x clone; APG = the aria-practices clone; HTML = the WHATWG HTML standard, popover light dismiss (https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/popover.html#popover-light-dismiss); P44 = the [Prototype: Anchored pane positioning, measured Positionable port versus CDK Overlay](44-prototype-anchored-pane.md) answer and `prototypes/anchored-pane/`; R:x = the research files by the short names `building-blocks.md` uses; BB = `building-blocks.md`; TRG = [Spec: Triggers (shared utility)](54-spec-triggers.md) and `specs/triggers.md`; WCAG = WCAG 2.2 AA success criteria as the user's rule names them.

Round 1 (no prerequisites): what Foundation does and what is settled.

1. **What is Foundation's positioning contract?** Positionable owns `position`, `alignment`, `allowOverlap`, `allowBottomOverlap`, `vOffset`, `hOffset` and delegates to Box: document-coordinate formulas (`GetExplicitOffsets`), a spill-over magnitude (`OverlapArea`, bound = `document.body`'s box at the current scroll with an asymmetric right edge), a search that cycles alignments then positions `left, right, top, bottom`, least overlap if nothing fits, and `$.fn.offset()` to write inline `top`/`left`. No public methods. Source: FS `js/foundation.positionable.js:5-205`, `js/foundation.util.box.js:22-173`; R:positioned "Shared positioning model".
2. **Which consumers, and what does each need?** Dropdown pane: all six Options plus `parentClass`, anchors from `registerTrigger`, `hover`/`hoverPane`/`hoverDelay` 250, `closeOnClick`, `autoFocus`, `trapFocus`, `has-position-*` classes (FS `foundation.dropdown.js:52-120, 165-237, 332-433`). Tooltip: the six with `allowBottomOverlap: false`, `tooltipHeight` 14/`tooltipWidth` 12, `hoverDelay` 200, `disableHover`, `disableForTouch`, `clickOpen`, pip classes (FS `foundation.tooltip.js:85-99, 141-142, 198-265, 308-420`). DropdownMenu: `Box.ImNotTouchingYou($sub, null, true)` (left plus right spill-over against the body box) to flip `opens-*`, `hoverDelay` 50, `closingTime` 500, `disableHover`, `disableHoverOnTouch`, `closeOnClick` (FS `foundation.dropdownMenu.js:302-328`; R:menus 3.3, 3.5). BB Tables A to C.
3. **What do BB, ADRs, and P44 already settle?** ADR 0002: in-place measured positioning, no popover or anchor positioning (out of target). ADR 0008: first paint from host bindings, browser work in render callbacks, timers outside the zone, hover via non-replayed `pointerenter`. BB 1.5: services only for shared state, `@Service()`. BB Part 3 and TRG decision 15: Light dismiss ignores presses on registered Triggers. P44: adopt the port, fresh search every open, one measurement, `ResizeObserver` on pane, anchor, and `body`, placement as a signal, `followScroll` recommended, two Foundation bugs not ported, CDK not the fallback. Reopened here with reasons: BB Table C's "Positioner service" (decision 5) and ADR 0002's fallback (decision 23).

Round 2 (depends on 1-3): shape and DI.

4. **One utility or three?** Three parts in one entry point: Positioner, Light dismiss registry, hover intent. They are separable (DropdownMenu uses Light dismiss and hover intent without the Positioner; a consumer could position without dismissing), but ship together because every consumer uses at least two. Source: BB Part 3 item 3 and Table C; R:positioned Dropdown and Tooltip dependency lists.
5. **Positioner: `@Service()` or per-pane helper?** A per-pane injection-context function, `nfsPositioner(options): NfsPositionerRef`. Its state (measurement, observer, scroll listener, placement) is per pane; the shared "placement rules" are pure functions with no state to share. A root service would need per-pane registrations to hold that state. Precedents: CDK `createFlexibleConnectedPositionStrategy(injector, origin)` (R:cdk overlay), Material `_animationsDisabled()` (R:di 5), P44 `anchoredPositioner()`. This amends BB Table C ("Positioner service ... (`@Service()`)"; proposed change below).
6. **Light dismiss: service?** Yes, `NfsLightDismiss` with `@Service()`: the stack of open entries and one set of document listeners are shared across instances, the case BB 1.5 reserves services for. Helper `nfsLightDismiss(options)` registers one entry while open. CDK's `OverlayOutsideClickDispatcher` is a `@Service()` for the same reason (NGC `cdk/overlay/dispatchers/overlay-outside-click-dispatcher.ts`).
7. **Hover intent: helper, directive, or host listeners?** Helper, `nfsHoverIntent(options)`. The Hover region spans the Triggers and the pane, which are different elements owned by different directives, so host listeners on one element cannot express it; `pointerenter` is not replayed anyway, so code-added listeners lose nothing (R:rendering 4 "Which events replay").
8. **Tokens, Defaults token, provider function?** None. The utility has no Foundation Options of its own; consumers keep theirs and their Defaults tokens (BB 1.4); a single service needs no `provide*` wrapper (R:di 5 "`provideXxx()` functions"; BB 1.9). RTL comes from `Directionality`, so `[dir]` scoping works without a token.
9. **Names?** `nfsPositioner`, `NfsPositionerRef`, `NfsLightDismiss`, `nfsLightDismiss`, `nfsHoverIntent`, pure `nfsExplicitOffsets`, `nfsOverlap`, `nfsPlace`, `nfsResolvePlacement`, `nfsBodyBounds`, `nfsRectBounds`, `nfsDocumentRect`, types `NfsPosition`, `NfsAlignment`, `NfsPlacement`, `NfsRect`, `NfsBounds`, `NfsDismissReason`. The `Nfs`/`nfs` prefix per ADR 0009; function names follow the Breakpoint service's `nfsBreakpointForWidth` form. Glossary term Positioner kept.
10. **Link to Triggers?** The consumer's `registerTrigger` set feeds Light dismiss (inside test) and hover intent (region); the anchor is the element passed to `open(trigger)`, else the first registered Trigger. Source: TRG API table (`open(trigger?)`, `registerTrigger`); FS `foundation.dropdown.js:119, 131`.

Round 3 (depends on 4-10): the Positioner.

11. **Options and defaults?** `open`, `anchor`, `position` and `alignment` (`'auto'`), `autoPosition` (default `bottom`), `autoAlignment` (default: the RTL-aware base rule), `vOffset`, `hOffset` (0), `pip` (0 x 0), `allowOverlap` (false), `allowBottomOverlap` (true), `boundary` (null), `followScroll` (true), all as functions so signals and inputs pass straight in. Defaults are Positionable's (FS `foundation.positionable.js:158-205`); Tooltip passes `autoPosition: 'top'`, `autoAlignment: 'center'`, `pip` 14 x 12, `allowBottomOverlap: false` (FS `foundation.tooltip.js:67-99, 420`). No `kind` switch: the two consumer differences are just values.
12. **`auto` resolution and legacy classes?** Positionable's rule: `auto` position is `autoPosition`; `auto` alignment is `autoAlignment` when given, else `left` (`right` in RTL) for top/bottom and `bottom` for left/right (FS `foundation.positionable.js:45-58`). The utility does not read Foundation's legacy `.top`/`.float-right` classes; the Dropdown and Tooltip specs decide whether to keep them as defaults, since BB 1.4 drops class-name Options but these are documented Foundation markup (R:positioned Dropdown 2, Tooltip 2).
13. **Search and measurement?** Foundation's order and least-overlap rule, candidates computed from one measurement, tried set fresh on every run. P44 measured the port equal to Foundation to 0.02 px on every fixture in three engines, and showed Foundation's per-candidate measuring causes the 19.19 px tip gap and its kept tried set leaves panes overflowing after one nothing-fits open. Not ported: both bugs (R:positioned "Reposition search" names the second a quirk).
14. **Collision bound?** Body box by default, with Foundation's asymmetric right edge (no `scrollX`), and a `boundary` element for Dropdown's `parentClass` (`nfsRectBounds`). The viewport alternative was triaged and decided (Triage 1). Source: FS `foundation.util.box.js:84-98`; P44 body-box cases; ADR 0002.
15. **RTL?** `Directionality.valueSignal()` from `@angular/cdk/bidi`, never `html[dir]` (BB 1.5); `auto` alignment only; explicit positions stay physical, as Foundation's docs say ("'right' still means right", FS `docs/pages/tooltip.md:79-81`; P44 RTL cases). Material's logical `before`/`after` not offered: Foundation's vocabulary is physical.
16. **Phases?** `earlyRead` measures the anchor rect, pane size, containing-block origin (pane rect minus computed `top`/`left`, `offsetTop`/`offsetLeft` fallback), scroll, and bound; `write` resolves, searches, writes, and sets `placement`. Source: P44 `anchored-pane.ts`; NG `render3/reactivity/after_render_effect.ts:318-390` (phase contract). `afterRenderEffect` never runs on the server.
17. **Write channel: inline styles, `--nfs-*`, or host bindings?** Inline `top`/`left` through `Renderer2.setStyle` in `write`. It is Foundation's own mechanism (`$.fn.offset()` writes inline `top`/`left`, R:positioned "Placement formulae"), needs no library CSS (a `--nfs-*` property needs a `top: var(...)` rule Foundation does not need, against the reuse rule, BB 1.13), and lands before paint; a host style binding from a signal would work too but adds a binding pass for no gain. BB 1.5 allows `Renderer2` writes for measured values.
18. **Does the placement class land before paint?** Yes: the `placement` write inside a render callback marks the consumer's view dirty, and `ApplicationRef.tick()` re-runs change detection before returning while views are dirty (up to 10 passes), so classes and offsets reach the same frame. Source: NG `application/application_ref.ts:153, 594-609` (`MAXIMUM_REFRESH_RERUNS`). Hence no `.is-opening` state (P44 decision for the Dropdown spec).
19. **`ResizeObserver` targets and lifetime?** Pane, anchor, and the bound element (`body` or `boundary`), created lazily in the first open run, observing only while open, disconnected on close and destroy. Replaces debounced `resizeme.zf.trigger` (FS `foundation.util.triggers.js:167-222`). Delta over P44 (which kept observing while closed): a page with many tooltips then holds no observers for closed ones.
20. **What re-runs, and does scroll re-search?** Open and `reposition()`: full search. Anchor or bound resize: full search. Pane resize: full search with a loop guard (two consecutive pane-resize runs alternating between the same two placements keep the current one until the next open, anchor or bound resize, or `reposition()`); P44 lists resize loops as unproven, so the guard is a cheap safeguard and an e2e case asserts it. Scroll with `followScroll`: re-offset in the current placement, no search: it fixes the static-scroller detachment P44 measured in Foundation and the port, and never flips a pane mid-scroll. Rejected: re-searching on scroll (CDK's reposition strategy and anchor positioning do, but Foundation chooses at open time and a flip during scroll is jarring).
21. **`followScroll` default?** On. It only corrects a detachment Foundation also has (P44 "static scroller" case), costs one passive captured listener while open, and changes nothing where no static scroller sits between pane and containing block. Consumers may expose it as an input; listed as a delta in their specs.
22. **`reposition()` and `placement`?** `reposition()` covers the one case no observer sees, an anchor that moves without resizing (P44 "does not prove"), as Material's `updatePosition()` does (R:material 5). `placement` is `null` until the first run and keeps its last value after close, so a leave animation keeps its pip and position.
23. **CDK Overlay as fallback?** No. P44: viewport bound, no `allowBottomOverlap`, `cdkScrollable` needed on static scrollers, `.tooltip` off by 209 to 254 px in 5 placements, the tip's `max-width` replaced, no pane in server HTML; the port does not fail inside scrolling ancestors, which was the only condition for the fallback. Proposed ADR 0002 amendment below.
24. **Containing block and documented limits?** The pane's containing block is its nearest positioned ancestor; for the tip (the trigger's next sibling, BB 1.1 case 3) the trigger's nearest positioned ancestor, not `.has-tip`. A positioned `overflow` ancestor clips an in-place pane (identical to Foundation) and tip (a regression, Triage 2); an ancestor stacking context bounds the tip's `z-index` (same cause). Untested and documented: `transform`/`filter`/`contain` ancestors, fixed or sticky ancestors, zoom, horizontal page scroll (P44 "does not prove").
25. **Dev-mode checks?** Same-axis alignment (Foundation treats it as center), negative offsets (can cover the trigger, WCAG 2.4.11), and a placed element whose computed `position` is not `absolute` (missing export mixin). Only under `ngDevMode`, in a render callback.

Round 4 (depends on 4-10): Light dismiss.

26. **Pointer rule: `pointerdown`, `click`, or down plus up?** A capture `pointerdown` records the target; the next `pointerup` decides; `pointercancel` clears. Both outside dismisses. This is the HTML popover light-dismiss algorithm (HTML), so a touch scroll (which ends in `pointercancel`) and a selection dragged out of the pane never dismiss, and no iOS `click`-delegation workaround is needed. Rejected: `pointerdown` alone (dismisses on scroll swipes); CDK's `click` with a recorded `pointerdown` origin (NGC `overlay-outside-click-dispatcher.ts`, which sets `cursor: pointer` on `body` for iOS); Foundation's body `click`/`tap` (FS `foundation.dropdown.js:223-237`). Recorded as the proposed ADR.
27. **What is inside?** The pane, its registered Triggers (so a Trigger click toggles, TRG decision 15, FS `foundation.dropdown.js:228`), and every open descendant entry.
28. **Nesting?** Ancestry by containment of the child's pane or of one of its Triggers, evaluated at event time: the popover invoker rule (HTML; R:platform 2 "Nesting"). A press walks the stack from the top and keeps an entry that contains the target or contains a kept entry; the rest close. So a press in a parent closes its children and keeps it; a child written elsewhere but opened from a Trigger inside the parent still counts as its child.
29. **Escape?** Document `keydown`, bubble phase, `key === 'Escape'`, no modifier (`hasModifierKey`, BB 1.5), not composing, not `defaultPrevented`; closes the topmost entry only (HTML: close requests act on the topmost popover; APG disclosure navigation: Escape closes the open dropdown), then calls `preventDefault()` last. Document level, because WCAG 1.4.13 needs hover content dismissible without moving focus onto it, which Foundation's host `keydown` on trigger and pane cannot do. Checked for this ticket with Playwright 1.63 in Chromium 153, Firefox 155, and WebKit 26.6: a prevented `keydown` on the document keeps a `showModal()` dialog open and fires no `cancel`; the next Escape closes it; a `show()` dialog has no Escape behaviour of its own. So a pane inside a modal Reveal closes first without closing the Reveal.
30. **Focus leaving?** Always on: a document `focusin` outside an entry closes it (`'tab'`, or `'click'` while a press is in progress). Sources: BB 1.10 ("moving focus out of an overlay closes it"), APG disclosure navigation (focus leaving closes an open dropdown, R:apg DropdownMenu), WCAG 2.4.11. `focusin` rather than `focusout`: clicking a non-focusable area inside a pane and leaving the window fire no `focusin`, so neither closes. No opt-out: Dropdown's `trapFocus` keeps focus inside anyway. Delta: Foundation has no focus rule.
31. **Sibling groups?** A `group` string per entry (`'nfs-dropdown-pane'`, `'nfs-tooltip'`, menus choose theirs); adding an entry closes open entries of its group that are not its ancestors. Replaces `closeme.zf.*` (FS `foundation.util.triggers.js:123-155`), which queried every instance of the plugin and closed parents too. Per-kind groups keep Foundation's scope; one global group (native `auto`) would let a tooltip close a dropdown.
32. **Per-entry switches?** Only the pointer rule (`outsidePress`): Dropdown's `closeOnClick` (default false in Foundation) and Tooltip's `clickOpen` map to it. Escape, focus, and siblings always apply, as accessibility requirements (BB 1.10; WCAG 1.4.13, 2.4.11).
33. **Close reasons?** `'click' | 'keydown' | 'tab' | 'sibling'`: Material's `MenuCloseReason` words (R:material 5; BB Table B Dropdown's `closed` reason) plus the sibling case. Focus rule for consumers: return focus to the Trigger only on `'keydown'` with focus inside the pane (APG: Escape returns focus to the trigger); move none otherwise.
34. **Listener lifetime and zone?** Five document listeners (`pointerdown` and `pointerup` and `pointercancel` capture, `keydown` bubble, `focusin`), attached outside the Angular zone when the first entry is added and removed after the last; entries are added only from render callbacks, so none exist on the server or before hydration. Source: BB 1.5 (`NgZone` only for `runOutsideAngular`); R:rendering 7 rules 3, 5, 6.

Round 5 (depends on 4-10): hover intent.

35. **Mechanics?** `pointerenter`/`pointerleave` added in code on each Trigger and the pane, from an `afterRenderEffect` following the element lists; open timer on entering a Trigger, close timer on leaving the whole region, cancelled on re-entry; `setTimeout` outside the zone, started only in handlers (BB 1.11 decision 4). Replaces jQuery `mouseenter`/`mouseleave` with `ignoreMousedisappear` (FS `foundation.core.utils.js:114-144`), which is dropped: leaving to browser chrome now closes after the delay like any leave.
36. **Touch?** `pointerType === 'touch'` is ignored per event; mouse and pen open. Replaces `what-input` (FS `foundation.dropdown.js:170-171`) and the device-level `disableHoverOnTouch`/`disableForTouch`, which turn mouse hover off on touch-screen laptops (R:menus 3.3; R:positioned Tooltip 3). Whether those Options survive is the consumer specs' call.
37. **WCAG 1.4.13 hoverable and persistent?** The pane is part of the Hover region (consumers must pass it), and the close delay has a 100 ms floor so the pointer can cross the pip gap; there is no auto-hide timer; pending timers clear whenever the state changes by another path, so a dismissed pane does not reopen until the pointer re-enters. Foundation's Tooltip closed on `mouseleave` immediately and ignored the tip (FS `foundation.tooltip.js:209-214`, "Nothing is bound to the tip element itself"); Dropdown's `hoverPane: false` default leaves the pane out (FS `foundation.dropdown.js:360`); both fail 1.4.13, so the Dropdown spec must flip `hoverPane` or drop it and the Tooltip spec picks a close delay at or above the floor.
38. **Focus opening and replay?** A consumer's `focusin` host listener that opens (Tooltip) re-checks `document.activeElement` against its host first, because a replayed `focusin` can arrive after focus moved (R:rendering 4 "Worked cases", last bullet; BB 1.11 decision 5). No attach-time `:hover` check: a sticky `:hover` after a tap on touch devices would open ghosts.

Round 6 (depends on 11-38): consumers, focus, ARIA, WCAG.

39. **DropdownMenu reuse?** Pure `nfsDocumentRect`, `nfsBodyBounds`, and `nfsOverlap(rect, bounds, {axis: 'horizontal'})` (Box's `lrOnly`). The flip is a signal written in `write`, so the second check runs in the same tick before paint (decision 18). The `opens-*` classes and `alignment: auto` (`.top-bar-right`, `align-right`, RTL) belong to the [Spec: Nested menu (shared utility)](56-spec-nested-menu.md). Source: FS `foundation.dropdownMenu.js:63-77, 302-328`; P44 hand-off.
40. **`autoFocus` and `trapFocus`?** Not in the utility (one consumer, YAGNI): the Dropdown pane spec applies `InteractivityChecker.isFocusable`/`isVisible` for the first element in DOM order (Foundation's tabindex sort dropped, positive tabindex being an anti-pattern) and CDK `FocusTrap` while open, never with `aria-modal` (R:apg Dropdown), Escape still closing (WCAG 2.1.2). Source: BB 1.2 CDK bullet; R:cdk a11y.
41. **ARIA the utility imposes?** None rendered. Consumers: no `aria-hidden` on panes (Foundation's CSS hides closed ones; BB 1.10), no `aria-modal`, focus return per decision 33, the pane in the Hover region, the replayed-`focusin` check, Trigger ARIA from TRG, `aria-describedby` on a tooltip host once the tip exists (BB Table B Tooltip).
42. **WCAG 2.2 AA criteria, and what the utility guarantees (coordinator's rule)?** 1.4.13: Escape from anywhere on the topmost entry, no reopen until re-entry, pane in the Hover region with a 100 ms close floor, no auto-hide. 2.4.11: every formula places the pane beside its anchor for non-negative offsets (dev warning otherwise), the focus rule closes a pane as focus moves past it, sibling groups keep one of a kind. 1.4.10: at 320 px the search tries all 12 placements and takes a zero-spill-over one whenever it exists; sizing to fit is the consumer's (Foundation's `.tiny`/`.small`, `$dropdown-width`), with an e2e case at 320 x 640. 2.5.8: the utility adds no targets and never covers its Trigger; axe's `target-size` rule runs in every story (ADR 0018 rule set). Also 1.3.2 and 2.4.3 (in-place panes keep DOM, reading, and focus order) and 2.1.2 (Escape and the focus rule always apply). Source: WCAG 2.2 as named by the user; R:apg Dropdown and Tooltip deltas; BB 1.10.
43. **Consumer mapping?** The spec's "What each consumer maps" table: Dropdown (six Options, `parentClass` to `boundary`, `closeOnClick` to `outsidePress`, `hover`/`hoverDelay` to both delays), Tooltip (`autoPosition` top, `autoAlignment` center, pip, `allowBottomOverlap` false, `clickOpen` to `outsidePress`, `hoverDelay` to `openDelay`), DropdownMenu (`hoverDelay` 50, `closingTime` 500, collision functions). Source: decisions 2 and 11.

Round 7 (depends on everything above): implementation level, rendering modes, Sass, tests.

44. **Implementation level?** Custom Angular with platform measurement (ADR 0002). `popover` (Firefox 125, iOS 18.3) and anchor positioning (Baseline 2026) are out of target (R:platform 2, 3); Aria has no pattern; CDK supplies only `Directionality` here (and `InteractivityChecker`, `FocusTrap`, `hasModifierKey` to consumers). No fallback needed (decision 23).
45. **Material and CDK comparison?** Borrowed: `MenuCloseReason` words, the Escape modifier rule (R:material 4), the capture-phase pointer origin (CDK dispatcher), `updatePosition()` as `reposition()`. Not borrowed: overlay containers, portals, scroll strategies, `cdkScrollable`, viewport bounds, push, `positionChange` output (a signal instead).
46. **Server output and first paint?** Nothing runs on the server; no inline offsets, no placement classes, no listeners; panes are in server HTML hidden by Foundation's CSS so `aria-controls` is valid (ADR 0002 Consequences; R:rendering 7 rules 1-3). An open-at-first-paint pane renders `.is-open` at its static position (below the trigger in Foundation's docs markup) and settles once in the first client render callback; rejected: `.is-opening` on the server, which would stay invisible forever inside `hydrate never` and to no-JS users (ADR 0008 first-paint rule).
47. **Event replay and hydration?** Document listeners never replay, correctly (nothing is open before hydration; R:rendering 4 "Global targets do not replay"). A pre-hydration Trigger click replays through the Trigger's host listener and the Positioner places the pane in the next render callback (R:rendering 4 Worked cases, Dropdown). Hover before hydration does nothing; the next entry opens. The Escape handler's `preventDefault()` never meets the replay throw because it is never replayed. No host bindings, so hydration compares nothing of the utility's.
48. **`@defer`?** No `@defer` in library code; inside a dehydrated block panes are closed server HTML and a click hydrates, replays, opens, places; `hydrate on hover` hydrates on the first hover without opening (the `pointerenter` already passed), so the spec recommends `on viewport`/`on idle` for blocks with hover-opened panes; `hydrate never` leaves panes closed. Trigger, pane, and parent pane share one boundary (ADR 0008 decision 6).
49. **Animation?** None; placement before paint lets enter keyframes start in place; kept offsets let leave keyframes finish in place; hover delays ignore `prefers-reduced-motion` (not motion); `reducedMotion` from the Breakpoint service is not read.
50. **Sass?** No library CSS and no `nfs-anchored-pane` mixin; it depends on `position: absolute` from the consumers' `foundation-dropdown`, `foundation-tooltip`, `foundation-dropdown-menu`; items (1) to (5) per [Sass packaging for the new library](57-sass-packaging.md) and ADR 0012.
51. **Testing seams?** Story ids `anchored-pane--<story>` with test consumers (a test pane directive and a test tip) that live with tests and stories; axe on the ADR 0018 WCAG 2.2 AA rule set with panes open; browser-level cases for phases, observer lifetime, loop guard, registry rules driven by dispatched events, hover timers, dev warnings, zoneless; node-level pure-formula tables and an SSR smoke (no offsets, no listeners, `whenStable()` resolves); Playwright e2e on `anchored-pane--fixture` through `mount(storyId, props)` for the 12 placements, fallbacks, body box, scrollers, resize, RTL, reflow at 320 px, the loop guard, the clipping limit, real mouse paths, touch emulation, Escape in a modal dialog, plus the fixture app for first paint, hydration, pre-hydration click, and hydrate triggers. Geometry is compared with the formulas and P44's recorded Foundation values; Foundation's JavaScript is not a test dependency. Source: BB 1.12; ADR 0018.
52. **Platform features when the target moves?** `popover="auto"` for Light dismiss (same model by decision 26), `popover="hint"` for tooltips, CSS anchor positioning for the Positioner (retires the body-box bound), interest invokers for hover, `CloseWatcher` for the Android back gesture (R:platform 2, 3; ADR 0002 Considered options).

Round 8 (added on the user's request; depends on 5, 6, 16, 18, 34, 35, 46, 48): loading and hooks.

53. **Load the Positioner and the registry lazily with `injectAsync`, or eagerly?** Eagerly. `injectAsync(loader, {prefetch: onIdle})` (`@publicApi 22.0`, NG `packages/core/src/di/inject_async.ts`; NG `adev/src/content/guide/di/lazy-loading-services.md`) resolves an auto-provided DI token (`@Service()` or `providedIn: 'root'`) from a dynamic import on first call. Weighed for, as the user asked: server rendering never opens a pane, and most panes are never opened, so the code is needed only on first show. Against, and deciding: (a) the Positioner is a per-pane injection-context function (decision 5), not a token, so `injectAsync` cannot return it without turning it back into a root service holding per-pane state; (b) placement must land in the same render pass that shows the pane (decision 18); an awaited load on first open either shows the pane unplaced for at least one frame or forces a hidden `.is-opening` state until the chunk arrives, and on a slow network the first Tooltip hover or Dropdown click waits on a fetch, which `prefetch: onIdle` shortens but does not remove (idle may not come before the first interaction on a busy page); (c) the registry's `add()` must run synchronously in the open render callback, or an Escape or outside press right after opening is missed and the first-open behaviour differs from every later one; (d) the whole utility is small (pure formulas, three helpers, one service) and a separate chunk costs a request and module overhead for little saving; (e) the code costs nothing at runtime on the server, where `afterRenderEffect` never runs, only bytes in the consumer's chunk; (f) splitting already happens where it pays: each plugin is its own entry point (ADR 0008 decision 7, BB 1.3), so a consumer's `@defer` around a Dropdown or Tooltip loads the utility with it, and an application without panes never includes it (`@Service()` is tree-shakable). Revisit only if bundle measurements in the new repository show the utility matters; consumers remain free to `injectAsync` their own heavy services.
54. **Which render hook for each job: `afterRenderEffect`, `afterNextRender`, or `afterEveryRender`?** `afterRenderEffect` for the Positioner (its phases re-run only when a tracked signal changes: `open`, the anchor, the options, the observer and scroll ticks, so a closed pane costs nothing), for `nfsLightDismiss` (tracks `isOpen` to add and remove the entry) and for `nfsHoverIntent` (tracks the element lists to attach and detach listeners). `afterNextRender` is not needed: nothing runs once regardless of state. `afterEveryRender` is rejected everywhere: it runs after every change detection in the application, so each pane on a page would re-measure (layout reads) on every keystroke anywhere, which is the cost Foundation's per-open measuring had and the signal-tracked hook avoids. Dev-mode checks run in the Positioner's own `earlyRead` under `ngDevMode`. Source: NG `render3/reactivity/after_render_effect.ts` (phases run "only when dirty through signal dependencies"); the angular-developer skill `effects.md` (phase split); BB 1.5.

### Proposed glossary terms

**Placement**:
One of the 12 pairs of a position (the side of its Trigger an Anchored pane sits on) and an alignment (the edge or centre that lines up), such as `bottom-left` or `top-center`, that the Positioner resolves.
_Avoid_: position (alone, which is one half), side, orientation, anchor point

**Collision bound**:
The box an Anchored pane's Placement must fit inside for the Positioner to accept it: the body box at the current scroll offset by default, as in Foundation, or a chosen ancestor.
_Avoid_: viewport (which it is not), window, boundary box, container

**Hover region**:
The Triggers of a hover-opened Anchored pane together with the pane itself; the pane stays open while the pointer is anywhere over it.
_Avoid_: hover area, hot zone, hover target

Amended term (replace the definition of **Light dismiss**): "Closing an Anchored pane or an open submenu on an outside pointer press, on Escape, when focus moves outside it, or when a sibling of the same kind opens; the replacement for Foundation's `closeme.zf.*` broadcast and body click handlers." Reason: decision 30 adds the focus rule, which the current definition omits.

### Proposed ADR

[adr/0024-anchored-pane-light-dismiss-model.md](../adr/0024-anchored-pane-light-dismiss-model.md): "Light dismiss follows the HTML popover light-dismiss model, not Foundation's body click". It meets the bar. Hard to reverse: every Anchored pane and submenu spec inherits the dismissal rules and close reasons, and consumers' tests assert them. Surprising: Foundation users expect body-click dismissal gated by `closeOnClick`, and Angular users expect CDK's click-based dispatcher; the down-plus-up rule and the always-on focus rule differ from both. A real trade-off: Foundation's rules, CDK's rule, `pointerdown` alone, and one global group were each weighed (decisions 26 to 32).
