## 1. Ruling summary

1. The evidence has 718 rows: 417 new since ticket 79, 297 re-checked, and 4 carried. The lenses gave a fails or partly verdict on 10 rows. The judge upholds 7 of those rows, as 4 items in 3 specs, and turns down the other 3. All other 711 rows hold. 38 of them carry a stated reason that is wrong, loose, or missing, and section 4 restates it. Nothing goes to the user.
2. One item sets a rule that later readers inherit. Foundation's base element styles still get no directive. But where a colour those rules print fails WCAG 2.2 AA on Foundation's defaults, the Library mixin of the export mixin that prints it checks the colour. `$blockquote-color` is the one such colour that nothing checks today (3.423:1). The map's base-styles line gains that sentence (section 5 of the ticket's Answer).
3. ADR 0045 is applied the same way on every row. A change that a later minor release can add costs little to defer: a new host, input, development check, recipe, or fix. A new compile error or a new required setting cannot be added later without breaking builds, so it waits for an Angular major and is settled now. That settles input-1 now. It also lets the File Upload Button's label host and an `ng add` entry wait.
4. "Additive later" says when a change could land. It does not say whether a record already owes it. ADR 0039 makes a development check part of a directive's contract where the directive's documented markup lacks it for WCAG 2.2 AA and nothing else reports the case. So the Switch's radio-group check comes in now, even though adding it later would be additive. No other "additive later" row is owed by a record.
