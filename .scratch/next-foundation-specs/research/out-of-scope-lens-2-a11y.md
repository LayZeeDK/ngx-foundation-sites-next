### 1.1 `$blockquote-color` (input-1, typography-helpers-1, map-1)

- `foundation-typography-base` prints `blockquote { &, p { color: $blockquote-color } }` (`FS/scss/typography/_base.scss:421-430`) with `$blockquote-color: $dark-gray` (`:188`): 3.423:1 on `$body-background` (`#fefefe`), the same pair the Typography Helpers' D9 measured failing axe `color-contrast` for `$subheader-color` and `$cite-color` (`specs/typography-helpers.md:9`, `:439`). Blockquote text is body-size text, so 4.5:1 applies.
- It is the one base-typography colour on Foundation's defaults that fails and that nothing checks: headings inherit (`$header-color: inherit`, `FS/scss/typography/_base.scss:43`), links are 4.647:1 (ADR 0022's dated note of 2026-09-28), `kbd` is 15.86:1, and `code`, `cite`, and heading `small` are checked (`specs/typography-helpers.md:525`).
- The exclusion's reason covers directives, not checks. ADR 0012 pairs a Library mixin with an export mixin, not with a class: "one Library mixin per export mixin, named after it and included after it, so each check runs only for a component the consumer compiles" (`adr/0012-sass-packaging.md:29`). `nfs-typography-base` is that mixin for `foundation-typography-base` (`specs/typography-helpers.md:523`), and the spec already checks colours that base element rules print: `$cite-color` is "also the look of every cite element" (`:536`), and `$header-small-font-color` colours `h1 small` to `h6 small` as well as `.h<n> small` (`FS/scss/typography/_base.scss:295`).
- The library's own gate fails without it: every story runs axe with `color-contrast` at `test = 'error'` (`specs/typography-helpers.md:361`); `typography-helpers--print-breaks` renders a `blockquote` of text and `--code-and-citations` a `cite` in a `blockquote` (`:369`, `:371`); the preview's overrides carry only three colours (`:357`, `:535-537`).
- ADR 0022 and the map leave no failing Foundation default as a recommendation (`adr/0022-wcag-2-2-aa-enforcement.md:7`; `map.md:42`), and D9 already rejected `@warn` for this grey (`specs/typography-helpers.md:439`).
- Impact MEDIUM: a fourth required setting and a compile stop for every consumer who includes `nfs-typography-base` on Foundation's defaults; before the first release it needs no deprecation (ADR 0045). Confidence HIGH: the measurement, ADR 0012's pairing, and D9 agree.
- Stays out: the blockquote's 1 px `$medium-gray` side border (about 1.6:1). 1.4.11 covers graphical objects needed to understand the content; the element's semantics and its indented text carry the quotation, not the border.
- Map line, proposed: after "a spec that relies on a base element style documents it" add "; where a colour those rules print fails WCAG 2.2 AA on Foundation's defaults, the Library mixin of the export mixin that prints it checks it (`nfs-typography-base` for `$header-small-font-color` and `$blockquote-color`)".

### 1.2 Radio switches without a group (switch-7)

- Foundation's radio switches sit in no group: three `input[type=radio][name=testGroup]` with screen-reader-only names and no `fieldset`, `legend`, or `radiogroup` (`FS/docs/pages/switch.md:67-100`). The Switch spec records this as Foundation's 1.3.1 failure and corrects its own examples (`specs/switch.md:91`, `:242`).
- ADR 0039: "Each directive also owns the ARIA, `type` default, or development-mode and compile-time check that its documented markup lacks for WCAG 2.2 AA" (`adr/0039-directives-manage-every-foundation-class.md:10`). P23 lists "a missing name" among the misuses a directive reports in development (`architecture-guide.md:324`). `input[nfsSwitchInput]` sits on the radio and can read its ancestry; axe 4.13 has no radio-group rule (`axe.js` defines no `radiogroup` rule id), so nothing else reports the case.
- "Additive later" (`specs/switch.md:410`; D16, `:442`) is not a reason ADR 0039 accepts. The Forms spec's parallel does not transfer: its radios carry no directive (`specs/forms.md:109`), and Foundation's Forms example already has its `fieldset` and `legend` (`FS/docs/pages/forms.md:153-157`).
- Impact LOW: development only, no public API. Confidence HIGH.

### 1.3 Grey text inside tinted containers (callout-7, card-7)

- The Typography Helpers' greys (with 1.1, four of them) are checked against `$body-background` only (`specs/typography-helpers.md:525`). On the container backgrounds the specs keep on Foundation's defaults, the required `#737373` falls under 4.5:1 for body-size text: 3.799:1 on the card divider (`$card-divider-background: $light-gray`, `FS/scss/settings/_settings.scss:354`; the Card spec keeps it, D9, `specs/card.md:308`), 3.870 to 4.307:1 on the five callout tints (`scale-color($color, $lightness: 85%)`, `FS/scss/components/_callout.scss:15`, `:71`), and 4.198:1 on the table's stripes and footer and 4.465:1 on its head (`smart-scale()` at 5 and 2.5 percent, `FS/scss/settings/_settings.scss:804-816`). The off-canvas panel and the top bar escape, because their specs require white (`specs/off-canvas.md:385`; `specs/top-bar.md:592`). A heading `small`, a subheader, a `cite`, or a blockquote in a card divider, a callout, or a table therefore fails 1.4.3 on the settings the specs require.
- card-7's reason is wrong as written: "`$header-small-font-color` is checked by ... `nfs-typography-base`" (`specs/card.md:286`) holds on the page, not on the divider, where Foundation's docs put the card's heading. callout-7 names "Foundation's base typography" (`specs/callout.md:324`), which no spec owns; headings themselves inherit the callout's checked text colour, so only the greys are open.
- What comes in: a requirement in the Typography Helpers spec (ADR 0022's dated note: a requirement on content the directives cannot read is stated as a requirement, `adr/0022-wcag-2-2-aa-enforcement.md:24`) with these ratios, naming `#666666` as the grey that holds on every one (4.601:1 on `#e6e6e6`, 4.686:1 on the alert tint, 5.084:1 on the stripes, 5.693:1 on the page). axe `color-contrast` reports each case wherever it renders, so no compile-time check is needed. Dark containers (`$titlebar-background: $black`, the tooltip) need a light grey instead; no one grey serves both.
- Alternative for the judge: a `@warn` in `nfs-callout`, `nfs-card`, and `nfs-table` naming the greys that fail on their backgrounds. An `@error` would stop compiles for greys a consumer never puts there.
- Impact LOW: a stated requirement and two reason edits. Confidence HIGH for the statement; the `@warn` is the judge's call.

### 1.4 The menu icon's bars under forced colours (top-bar-7)

- Under forced colours Foundation's bars vanish, and the Top Bar spec's transparent border leaves "a 24 px outlined box" (`specs/top-bar.md:263`). The control stays findable, but its only visible label, the three bars, is gone, so a contrast-theme user who is not running a screen reader sees an empty square. The Switch spec treats a control whose only visual disappears as the library's to fix (D12 rejects "No rule (an invisible control in contrast themes)", `specs/switch.md:438`), and so does the Slider's rule 13.
- The stated reason is wrong: drawing the bars restates no Foundation value. `foundation-menu-icon` calls `hamburger()` with its colours only (`FS/scss/components/_menu-icon.scss:1-9`), and `hamburger()` computes the bar offsets from its own default arguments (`FS/scss/util/_mixins.scss:83-143`). `nfs-menu-icon` can call the same mixin inside `@media (forced-colors: active)` with `CanvasText` (and `Highlight` on hover), with `forced-color-adjust: none` on `.menu-icon::after` so the `box-shadow` bars survive. The Switch's and Slider's objection to `forced-color-adjust: none` is that it keeps authored colours over the user's theme (`specs/switch.md:438`); with system colours it does not.
- Unmeasured here. A consumer who restyles `.menu-icon` with other `hamburger()` arguments gets default-size bars under forced colours.
- Impact LOW. Confidence NOT HIGH: forced colours are not a WCAG 2.2 AA criterion, the box already keeps the control findable, and the rule needs a measurement in Chromium and Firefox.

## 4. Holds whose stated reason is wrong or weak

- forms-6: "unmeasured" is not a reason. What holds: WCAG 2.2 AA has no forced-colours criterion, and a select stays identifiable by its border and text, which take system colours; the arrow is not its only visual, unlike the switch (1.4) or the menu icon's bars.
- responsive-embed-8: axe `video-caption` never fails a page. Its `caption` check returns "needs review" when no captions track exists (`axe.js:26190-26196`, `:33554-33561`), so the gate at `test = 'error'` cannot catch a missing track, and a `<video>` child, unlike a provider's frame, is visible to the directive. What holds: whether a video needs captions depends on whether it has speech, which no check can read; the recipes state the requirement (`specs/responsive-embed.md:26`, story 22 at `:51`), as ADR 0022's dated note asks.
- progress-bar-4: "which has no class (ADR 0039)" answers the directive, not the name check. What holds: a check-only directive on an element Foundation styles by tag runs only where the consumer already wrote it, so it reaches no one the recipe does not. The exclusion is safe only while the spec states the meter's name as a requirement: axe's `aria-meter-name` selects `[role="meter"]` only (`axe.js:32309-32311`), so an unnamed native meter, as in all three of Foundation's examples (`FS/docs/pages/progress-bar.md:137-139`), passes the gate. The spec's 4.1.2 row asks for a name on "every progress bar and native progress element" (`specs/progress-bar.md:270`) and enforces meters only through the play functions; its requirement column should name `<meter>` too.
- progress-bar-6: no reason is given. What holds: Foundation's progress partials declare no transition (none in `FS/scss/components/_progress-bar.scss`, `FS/scss/forms/_progress.scss`, or `FS/scss/forms/_meter.scss`), and the animation preference converts only Foundation's JavaScript animations (`map.md:43`).
- visibility-classes-9: the text the evidence quoted was stale (it said `.print-break-inside` lets an `img` break and that both classes were "proposed" for the Typography Helpers); the edits of [Re-run: Typography Helpers spec for the print helper classes](../issues/144-rerun-typography-helpers-print-classes.md) have since landed (`specs/visibility-classes.md:439`), and the reason as it now reads holds.
- badge-4: "a play function's name query" covers only the library's stories; axe `target-size` is what reaches a consumer's page. The verdict holds.
- The rows of section 1 whose reasons are also wrong: typography-helpers-1 and map-1 (a reason for no directive, not for no check), card-7 (the check it cites does not cover the divider), callout-7 (base typography has no owner), top-bar-7 (no offsets are restated).

## 5. Inclusion risks, if another lens brings these in

- The APG basic carousel (orbit-1): the slides must stop being tab panels, since a `tabpanel` without a `tab` is an orphan role, and become `group` slides with `aria-roledescription="slide"` and "N of M" names, so each Orbit would carry two ARIA contracts.
- The label-as-button file upload (forms-4, button-3): focus lands on a 1 px input (2.4.7). A fix needs the input before the label (`:focus-visible +`) or inside it (`:focus-within`), because `:has()` is outside the Browser target; the clipped input also hides the chosen file's name, so the recipe needs a visible output of it.
- A `closable` Callout input (callout-3): focus must leave the removed close button (2.4.3); the recipes move it in `closed` handlers, and an input would need a focus target rule.
- A `current` input on menu items or breadcrumbs (menu-4, breadcrumbs-3): a second source beside `aria-current`; the two can disagree, and the look and the announcement then split (ADR 0042).
- Default live roles on Badge, Label, and Callout (badge-2, label-2, callout-4): `role="status"` on every badge announces every count change, and `role="alert"` on server-rendered content interrupts at load in some readers.
- Swap announcements (responsive-accordion-tabs-11, and RM3 of [Triage the out-of-scope Foundation components and variants](../issues/79-triage-out-of-scope-components-and-variants.md)): speech while a user zooms, as in section 3.
- `focusMode="activedescendant"` for Tabs (TB5): the focused tab shows no browser ring, so 2.4.7 needs library CSS.
- Generated ids and English default names (switch-8, top-bar-5, close-button-2, RT6 of [Triage the out-of-scope Foundation components and variants](../issues/79-triage-out-of-scope-components-and-variants.md)): ids break references before hydration; English names read in the wrong language on other pages (3.1.2).
- Menu roles (menu-9, nested-menu-6, and G8 of [Triage the out-of-scope Foundation components and variants](../issues/79-triage-out-of-scope-components-and-variants.md)): only with the full APG key tables, and never on site navigation.
- Tooltips on non-interactive text or on a label (TT2 of [Triage the out-of-scope Foundation components and variants](../issues/79-triage-out-of-scope-components-and-variants.md), label-11): unreachable by keyboard and touch (2.1.1, 1.4.13).
- A width transition on the progress meter (progress-bar-6): it would need the reduced-motion override every library animation has.

## 6. Outside the rows

Not an exclusion, so not rated here; for the judge to route. Orbit's bullets are borderless buttons drawn with a background only (`FS/scss/components/_orbit.scss:137-151`, after Foundation's `button { border: 0; background: transparent }` reset, `FS/scss/_global.scss:223-231`), and the Orbit spec's rule 10 sizes them without a border (`specs/orbit.md:654`). By the mechanism the Switch spec measured (backgrounds replaced with the theme's colour in Chromium and Firefox, `specs/switch.md:12`), the bullets, the Orbit's required tab list, likely disappear under forced colours, the selected one included; the Orbit spec names no forced-colours behaviour. Unmeasured here. By the Switch's D12, an invisible control in contrast themes is the library's to fix, so a `CanvasText` border and a `Highlight` selected fill in `nfs-orbit` would match the Switch and the Slider. Impact LOW, confidence NOT HIGH until measured.
