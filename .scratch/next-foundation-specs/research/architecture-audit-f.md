## variant-declaration-tooling.md

No finding. This spec ships no directive, component, or attribute selector (it is Node/CommonJS workspace tooling: a generator, a sync generator, and a builder over Variant registries and a manifest), so the directive-shaped principles (P1-P13, P16, P17, P19-P21, P24-P26) do not apply, and the spec says so explicitly section by section ("ARIA and keyboard: None: nothing here renders"; "WCAG 2.2 AA: No criterion applies"). Checked hardest: P22 (entry-point and bundle discipline for the primary entry point's 26 registries and helpers, and the "never exported through exports" rule for both manifests -- meets it), P13 (registry, alias, and token naming against building-blocks 1.3's mechanical rule -- meets it), P18 (Sass packaging -- meets it; the spec correctly has no Library mixin of its own), and P24 (not applicable: no attribute-selector directive exists for `nfsDirectiveCheck` to sit on).

## Guide problems

None found in this group's specs. P24 is a decided rule (ADR 0046, "no longer provisional" per the guide's own text), not an open or untestable principle, and every spec in this group that falls short of it does so by omission (the required text and mechanism are absent), not because the principle cannot be met: `typography-helpers.md` already shows a spec author reasoning about P24 directly and reaching a mistaken conclusion about what satisfies it, which is evidence the principle is understandable and testable, not evidence against the guide.
