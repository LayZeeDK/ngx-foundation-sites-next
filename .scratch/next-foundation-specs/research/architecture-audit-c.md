## forgotten-import-checks

No finding. ## Findings about the guide itself

None found that rise to "no spec can meet it," "contradicts a record," or "untestable." The one recurring pattern worth the judge's attention without being filed as a guide problem: `float-classes-a2` and `float-grid-a2` both read a computed CSS `direction` for a development-only geometry check, a use P21 (restating building-blocks 1.5) does not list among its two recorded Slider exceptions. Because the reads never reach production and serve a different purpose (detecting a rendered mismatch, not choosing library behaviour) than the Slider's documented case, the judge may prefer to add a third recorded exception to building-blocks 1.5 rather than treat every future development check of this shape as a per-spec finding.
